Careers

Come build things that last

Working here

The work speaks. So do we.

Max Paving runs its own crews on its own equipment. The people we hire aren’t handed off to a sub or shuffled between companies: you work for us, alongside the same crew, on projects across the Kansas City metro.

The work is concrete, asphalt and the excavation underneath both. Driveways to distribution centers, and the municipal jobs in between. It’s outdoor trade work, busiest spring through fall, and at the end of a project you can drive past something that will outlast the truck you drove there.

We always want to hear from good people looking to further their career, whether that’s a laborer working toward finisher, a finisher working toward foreman, or an operator who wants more seat time on bigger work. If the crews are full the day you call, we’ll say so, keep your name, and reach out when there’s room.

Trades we hire

When crews have room

  • Heavy equipment operators
  • Concrete finishers and form setters
  • Asphalt laborers and rakers
  • CDL drivers
  • Experienced foremen

Equal opportunity

Everyone gets the same shot.

Max Paving is an equal opportunity employer. Employment decisions are based on qualifications, experience and work ethic, and on nothing else. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, sexual orientation and gender identity), national origin, age, disability, veteran status, genetic information, or any other status protected by federal, state or local law.

That policy covers every part of the job: hiring, pay, assignments, training, advancement, and how people are treated on our crews. If you need an accommodation to apply or to do the work, tell us when you call and we’ll work it out.
