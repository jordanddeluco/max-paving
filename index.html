Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every project starts with a free site assessment: we walk the property, measure it and talk through what you need. Then you get a written proposal with the scope, materials and full price, usually within a few business days.

What areas do you serve?

We work anywhere within 100 miles of our shop in Grain Valley, MO, which covers the full Kansas City metro on both the Missouri and Kansas sides. Not sure if we reach you? Call. We probably do.

Do you do both asphalt and concrete?

Yes, plus the excavation, base and drainage underneath both. All three trades are self-performed by our own crews, so you deal with one company and one contract.

Do you handle residential work?

Yes. Driveways, sidewalks, pads and repair for homeowners, built by the same crews, with the same materials and the same written guarantee as our commercial work.

Concrete or asphalt?

Both are good when they’re built right. Concrete costs more up front and lasts longer; asphalt costs less and needs more upkeep. It depends on your budget, how long you’ll own the property and what’s under the pavement now. We’ll give you both numbers if you want the comparison.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. Every concrete pour and asphalt lift carries a one-year written warranty on labor and workmanship, and it goes in the proposal, not in fine print. If something settles or cracks that shouldn’t within that year, call us and we fix it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Fully licensed and insured, with certificates of insurance and vendor onboarding paperwork ready for GCs, property managers and municipalities. We work for cities that require documented proof before a truck rolls.
