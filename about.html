About Max Paving

One family. Paving KC since 2014.

Our story

Family owned. One standard.

Max Paving is a family-owned concrete and asphalt contractor in Grain Valley, Missouri. Since 2014 we’ve been building parking lots, driveways and sidewalks around the Kansas City metro, along with the excavation and base work that comes before them. The company is younger than the people in it: our crew has more than 25 years in this trade.

We’re not the biggest contractor in town, and we’re fine with that. The work gets done by our own people, on our own equipment, and we’d rather keep it that way than grow past what we can stand behind. A homeowner’s driveway gets the same crews and the same attention as a warehouse truck lane.

2014 Founded

History of the company

Max Paving started in 2014 doing small private residential driveways. Despite decades of previous industry experience among the crew, a small and humble beginning laid the ground for the professional paving company it has become.

The work grew the way reputation grows in this trade: one finished job recommending the next, until the driveways became parking lots, the parking lots became commercial and municipal contracts, and the crew grew to match.

Above all, pride stems from the opportunity to take care of our team members and their families, as well as a number of non-profit organizations supporting local and international communities. Honesty and integrity run through the entire business.

“Making your project our mission.” The line we started with in 2014, and still the whole job description.
