Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what we do with it

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Max Paving (“we,” “us”) handles information collected through this website. We’re a concrete and asphalt contractor, not a data company: we collect what we need to respond to you and prepare a proposal, and we don’t sell it.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the quote request form, we receive what you enter: your name and phone number and, if you provide them, your email address, company, project address, the service and property type you select, your timeline and your project details. We also record which page of the site the request came from.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your request, schedule a site visit and prepare a proposal
  • To carry out and follow up on work you hire us for
  • To understand which pages lead people to contact us, so we can improve them
  • To keep business records and meet legal obligations

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don’t add you to a marketing list because you asked for a quote.

Who else handles it

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and the business, and only as needed for that purpose: our website host; FormSubmit, which delivers quote form submissions to our email inbox; our business email provider; and Google, which supplies the reviews linked from this site and the map on our contact page under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and project records for as long as we need them to respond to you, complete and support our work, and keep normal business and tax records. When we no longer need information, we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to records we’re required to keep. If you’d rather we didn’t contact you again about a request, tell us and we’ll stop.

Security

This site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we limit access to submitted information to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging construction work. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13.
