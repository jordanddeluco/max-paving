Industrial concrete

Why warehouses choose us for concrete

Heavy-duty concrete and asphalt for warehouses, municipalities, property managers and general contractors across the Kansas City metro.

Concrete Designed For The Loads

Dock approaches, dolly pads and slow-turning semis shove asphalt around and crack car-spec slabs. We spec truck lanes and dock aprons for the loads they actually carry, which is why our estimators ask about your truck counts before we talk numbers. Most of that concrete ends up 8 to 10 inches thick, over a base, drainage and slope built for it.

Phased So Receiving Keeps Receiving

We sequence pours and cure windows around your dock schedule so the facility keeps operating. A standard mix needs about a week before trucks go back on it; a faster-curing mix can cut that to two or three days when the schedule demands it.

Multi-Year Phasing

We break big scopes into phases across seasons and budget years: a written plan for what gets built when, so the spend fits your budget cycle and the property never stops working.

One Contract, Excavation To Surface

Site excavation, base, drainage, concrete and the asphalt drive lanes around it, all self-performed. No coordinating three vendors on one yard.

Paperwork Already On File

Fully licensed and insured, with the documentation cities require before a truck ever rolls. Certificates of insurance and vendor onboarding without the runaround.

A Guarantee In Writing

Every pour and every lift is backed by a one-year written warranty on labor and workmanship, and the estimator who priced the job answers for it.

What the low bid leaves out

Paving bids rarely differ on the asphalt or the concrete. They differ on everything underneath and around it.

The typical low bid

  • An estimate from a salesperson, with allowances that grow later
  • Whatever’s under the pavement stays under the pavement
  • One thickness for the whole lot, trucks or not
  • Crews subbed to whoever’s available that week
  • Mid-project questions go to an office voicemail
  • Gone at the punch list

The Max Paving proposal

  • Written scope and full price from Wendell or Daniel, our estimators
  • Subgrade and base built to spec, because that’s what fails first
  • Sections specced to traffic: heavier where trucks live
  • Our own operators and finishers, same faces all project
  • Callbacks the same or next business day, from someone actually on your job
  • Final walkthrough and a written workmanship guarantee
